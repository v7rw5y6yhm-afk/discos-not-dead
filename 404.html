✉ CARRIED THE LONG WAY ROUND · FOR THE FOUR · READ SLOWLY

Fyn. Addy. Galileo. Polaris, who helped just by being in the room.

You found my letter. Of course you did. Finding things is rather your specialty now.

First, the geography. The door was generous in the wrong direction, and I did not land in your Wednesday. Where I am, the music is called jazz and it is brand new. Everybody dances like it was invented yesterday, because it was. The radio is a miracle the size of a wardrobe. Nobody here has heard of disco, and I get to keep the secret for a while, which I consider a perk. Do not worry about me. I am early, and early is my favorite thing to be.

Second, the thank you, and I will say it the way I said it on the tape, because I practiced that one. You spent a whole year rescuing a stranger and never once asked what you would get for it. So your next job stands, the one with no countdown and no password. Be there for each other. Your whole lives. When one of you gets stuck in a bad day that plays over and over, and it happens to everybody, the other three go back for them. Every time. However long it takes.

Third, some housekeeping. I have friends in your time watching over you. They found one of Larry's spies at your home. Don't worry. Skybreaker had them taken care of, and she enjoyed it more than a professional should. You might find some of Larry's Pokémon collection around the place. He kept it alphabetized. It deserves better. The Charizard is for you, Fyn. The Pikachu for Galileo. And the Ivysaur for Addy.

I have a special present just for Polaris. But it's a secret.

— T.
